Frozen analysis plan, amended retrospectively (2026-10-01)¶

Pre-result registration is unverified. The first repository commit already included the plan, implementation and results. Statements inside the archived plan that it preceded all results or fixed splits beforehand lack independent timing evidence. Its SHA-256 proves content identity and does not establish chronology.

Current split policy: 2022-2023 are training; 2024 is pooled with training for fitting and baseline thresholds; 2025 is held out for the reported evaluation. The archived statement that validation is not used for final metrics does not represent the implemented policy. Rainfall-normalized ranking was not evaluated.

Archived original and unchanged hash are preserved byte-for-byte as historical evidence. This wrapper is the default reader-facing plan. Read results under the report-row estimand and conditional bootstrap limitations, without a confirmatory preregistration claim.
